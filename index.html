Two ways to store the same patient

Left: a flat registry row — one line per patient, easy to read, perfect for one hospital. Right: the OMOP Common Data Model — the same facts split into standard tables with standard concept IDs, so the same query runs at every hospital.

From raw lab values to registry fields

The registry does not store "CKD 3". It stores the raw numbers and calculates the stage the same way at every site: eGFR by CKD-EPI 2021, KDIGO G and A categories.

CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free): 142 × min(Scr/κ,1)α × max(Scr/κ,1)−1.200 × 0.9938age × 1.012 [female]. Inker LA et al. NEJM 2021.
